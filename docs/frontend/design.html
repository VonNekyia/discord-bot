Terranova · Design guide

Built like the world it belongs to.

The look of Terranova: warm parchment and deep forest, gold for what matters, serif for the story and a quiet sans for the work. This page is made from the design it describes; every colour, size and element below is shown as it really looks.

01 · Character

Warm, calm, crafted.

A medieval world built block by block. The design should feel like that world at its best: pictures carry the mood, the interface stays out of their way.

  1. 01

    Warm, not loud

    Cream and paper grounds, ink-green darks, gold accents. No pure black, no saturated interface colours.

  2. 02

    Serif tells, sans works

    Large serif headlines carry the voice; body text, labels and controls use a clean sans.

  3. 03

    Pictures first

    Renders and screenshots get room and as little veil as legibility allows.

  4. 04

    Faithful to the game

    Pixel art stays on its grid; figures wear real skins; textures are the game's own.

  5. 05

    The same for everyone

    Every language gets the same page; everything works without scripts and without third parties.

02 · Colour

Parchment, forest and gold.

Fourteen named colours, each with one job. Use the name, never the raw value; a new colour needs a name before its third use.

Grounds

  • cream

  • card

  • ink-soft

Accents and text

  • gold-light
    #f1d58dThe one primary action, selected states, emphasis on dark.
  • umber
    #8a652bGold that reads on light: numbers, kickers, eyebrows on paper.
  • sage

  • sage-light
    #cfe0c4Builder tags, success grounds, "do" examples.
  • muted
    #536158Secondary text on paper, cream and card.

Pairings

Every text colour has grounds it may stand on. Ratios are measured (WCAG 2): 4.5 is the floor for body text, 3 for large headlines.

Lines, shadows, veils

  • Lines ink at 15 % on light, white at 18 % on dark. One pixel, never darker.
  • Shadow one soft, deep shadow for floating panels (0 24 70, ink at 16 %); small objects get a two-step shadow instead.
  • Veils over pictures are the ink colour (8, 15, 10) at varying strength, never black: they read as dusk, not dirt. See Imagery.

04 · Space & shape

Room to breathe.

A content column of 1180 px, generous section padding, a short spacing scale and soft, consistent corners.

Layout

  • Column 1180 px; side gutters at least 24 px, 20 px on phones.
  • Sections 90–150 px top and bottom, grounds alternate paper, cream and a dark.
  • Gaps 18 px between cards, 8–14 px inside a group, 30 px between header items.
  • Grids 4–6 columns on wide screens, 2 on tablets, 1 on phones; an odd last item takes the row.
  • Breakpoints 460 (small phones), 760 (phones), 960 (tablets), 1100 (laptops), 1200 and 1600 (wide).
  • Text never runs wider than about 70 characters.

06 · Imagery & motion

Let the pictures speak.

Renders that look crafted, pixel art on its grid, veils only where text needs them, and motion that never gets in the way.

Veils

Pixel art

  • Pixel art is drawn on its own grid and shown only at whole multiples of it, never rotated or blurred.
  • Renders: warm key light, cool fill, soft bloom; real skins; the game's textures; transparent figures fade their shadow out instead of ending in a line.
  • Screenshots are cropped to the scene, without interface; the brightest part sits away from text.
  • Every picture has its width and height, a modern format and loads late below the fold; large ones come in several sizes so each screen gets what it needs.

Motion

Hover: lift 4 px · 180 ms
Reveal: fade in once · 650 ms
Crossfade: pictures · 1200 ms

Motion explains, it never decorates for its own sake. Anything that moves by itself can be paused, and the system setting for reduced motion turns it all off.

07 · Voice & practice

Speak plainly, build honestly.

German first and addressed with du, English mirrors it with you. Short sentences, plain words, and a page that respects whoever opens it.

Do

Hier ist jeder willkommen. Wir sind freundlich zueinander.

Don't

Diskriminierung wird nicht toleriert und streng sanktioniert.

Do

Updates kommen, wenn sie fertig sind.

Don't

Das ultimative Next-Level-Erlebnis wartet auf dich!!!

  • Say who is welcome, not who is not; values so a child understands them.
  • Headlines end with a full stop; labels are nouns.
  • Every language gets the same page, written, not machine-translated.
  • Contrast at least 4.5 : 1 for text; gold never as text on light.
  • Focus always visible; icon-only controls carry a label.
  • Works without scripts; scripts only add comfort.
  • Nothing from third parties: no web fonts, no trackers, no embedded players.
  • Privacy by construction: a visitor's data goes to us only, and only when they send it.
  • Every size from 320 px up, without sideways scrolling.
  • Not obvious? Write it down, with the reason, where the next person will look.
